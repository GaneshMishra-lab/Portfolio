## Why I think in layers

When systems get larger, complexity does not arrive all at once. It grows as small assumptions stack over time. I design with layers so each assumption has a clear boundary.

## The three checks I run

1. **Flow check**: Can data move through the system without hidden coupling?
2. **Stress check**: What fails first under load or partial outages?
3. **Learning check**: Will this architecture make future decisions easier, not harder?

## Practical outcome

By mapping user-facing goals to architecture constraints early, teams avoid reactive rewrites and can ship with confidence. I treat architecture as a growth system, not a one-time diagram.
